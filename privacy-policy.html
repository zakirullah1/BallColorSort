Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how TechZam Solution (Private) Limited ("TechZam", "we", "us") handles information in our mobile apps, including Color Ball Sort (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number inside the App. The App and its partners may collect:

  • Device and usage data: device model, operating system version, language, app version, crash logs and basic gameplay events.
  • Advertising ID and IP address: collected by Google AdMob to show and measure ads.
  • Purchase information (if purchases are added): handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation of a purchase but never your payment card details.

2. How we use information

  • To run the App, fix bugs and improve gameplay.
  • To show ads that keep the App free.
  • To process purchases and restore them when needed.
  • To respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner, interstitial, rewarded and app open ads through Google AdMob. Google may use your advertising ID and similar data to serve ads, including personalized ads where permitted. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

You can control ads on your device: on Android go to Settings > Privacy > Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization. Where required by law, the App shows a consent message before personalized ads are served.

4. Third-party services

The App uses Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play services. These services have their own privacy policies, and we do not control them. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and security

Gameplay progress is stored on your device. Data collected by third-party services is kept under their policies. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
